# Week two and after: a plan that fits the archive

By the creative director, 2026-11-12. For the code session to turn into rules, and for the
owner to see what the calendar can honestly hold. Figures are editorial choices I would
defend, not measurements; none has been tested on this account yet.

## What there is

Photographs of the house that may open or close a piece (fit 60 or more, fill the page,
not excluded, not finger food): seven.

| Service | Photographs | Used in week one |
|---|---|---|
| Chef at home | 121, 164, 126, 143 | a7 (121), a9 (164, 126, 143) |
| Cooking class | 106, 198 (crop), 199 (crop, middle only) | a10 (all three) |

Week one shows every one of them once. Nothing new arrives until guests send photographs
with written permission (docs/proposals/guest-photos.md).

## 1. Rest: the figures

A profile is read by its top rows. The same picture twice in the first three rows says the
house has little to show; the same cover twice says it has nothing new to say.

| Case | Rest | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A photograph of the house, in any place inside a piece | 10 days and 3 published pieces, whichever is longer | It is then out of the row of the grid where it last appeared |
| A cover returning as a cover | 28 days and 12 published pieces | Four rows of the grid: nobody scrolling the profile sees it twice |
| A former cover returning inside a piece | as any photograph: 10 days and 3 pieces | Inside, it is a detail of another story |
| The same cover line | never twice | A line is said once |
| A licensed picture of Sicily | 28 days and 12 pieces, in any place | There are fourteen of them; no reason to repeat |
| A crop and its source | count as one photograph | Already in the pool as one subject |

Count published pieces, not calendar slots: an empty day rests nothing.

What these figures do to the calendar, starting from week one (Friday 13, Sunday 15,
Monday 16 November): no photograph of the house is free before Monday 23 November (121),
Wednesday 25 (the class pictures) and Thursday 26 (164, 126, 143). So Wednesday 18,
Friday 20 and Sunday 22 stay empty unless the owner chooses otherwise. After that the
archive carries about two pieces a week, each built on returning photographs in new places.
Four a week is not possible with this archive under any rest I would defend; three a week
means the same seven pictures every ten days.

If the owner prefers presence over rest, the only figure I would loosen is the first:
7 days and 2 pieces. I would not loosen the cover rule.

## 2. The piece led by guests' words (A5)

Four slides. One photograph of the house.

1. Cover: the house's photograph, full page, with a line that promises the experience and
   the small line naming service and place. I would put the photograph here, not at the
   close: a cover of words alone does not stop anyone, and the sentences that follow then
   read as what was said about that plate.
2. One sentence from a guest, on the cream ground, set large. Attribution below in the
   small sans.
3. A second sentence from another guest, same setting. Optional: with only one good
   sentence the piece is three slides.
4. Invitation: wordmark, "Request availability on risthome.it", and the proof once, flat:
   "5.0 on Google, 101 reviews."

Form of a quoted sentence:
- one sentence, two at most, 25 words or fewer, word for word; it may be cut only at the
  end of a sentence, never inside one, and nothing is added;
- written in English by the guest; no translations;
- attribution: first name or initial, the source and the date: "Sarah, Google review,
  June 2026"; never a surname, never a photograph of the guest;
- about the service the piece shows (a sentence about the class does not sit under a
  dinner).

Before a sentence may be used, all of these are true:
1. it is on the house's public Google profile on the day of use, and the text matches
   word for word (keep the link and the date checked);
2. the owner has approved that sentence by name;
3. the guest received nothing in exchange for the review;
4. it names no other person, no child, no health matter, no price;
5. if the review disappears or the guest asks, the piece is withdrawn.
This is editorial care, not legal advice; whoever follows privacy for the house should
confirm that quoting a public review with a first name needs nothing more.

For the code session: guests write words the house's own voice may not use ("amazing",
"best"). The banned vocabulary governs what the house says, so a quoted field should be
exempt from it, and marked as a quote so the exemption cannot be borrowed by other text.
A store for approved sentences (text, first name, date, link, service, approved by, date
checked, withdrawn) would let the gate refuse anything not in it; `brand-facts.json`
forbids reviews "not listed above" today, so the store is where they become listed.

Rhythm: at most one such piece in seven, as the calendar already says for pieces that ask
for a booking.

## 3. A day when nothing can be built

Confirmed: leave the slot empty and say why in the console ("no photograph of the house is
rested: next free on <date>"). Do not repeat a photograph early, do not fall back to a
weaker one, and do not let licensed pictures carry a piece alone. Two additions:

- say the next date on which a piece can be built, so the owner sees the gap coming;
- when three slots in a row are empty, show it as a standing notice rather than three
  separate lines: that is the moment the owner should decide about guest photographs or a
  looser rest.

| If | Then |

| The owner approves ten sentences from Google | Up to five A5 pieces over ten weeks, each needing one rested photograph |
| None of the above | Two pieces a week from Monday 23 November, the same seven photographs in rotation |
