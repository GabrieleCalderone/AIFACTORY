## Not adopted, and why

- **The ready-made caption**: 600 characters with words the house standard bans ("exceptional", "memorable", "beautiful", "luxury"). The idea is kept, the wording follows the standard.
- **Concept A as storyboarded** (guests at the table, the chef among them): the archive has no photographs of guests with recorded consent. The format is approximated with the table, the chef and the dishes; the document itself says not to use synthetic images for it.
- **Two reels and one carousel a week**: the owner decided four posts a week on 2026-11-11; with photographs only, the carousel leads.
- **The 01:00 test window**: not scheduled until the first posts show where the reach comes from.
